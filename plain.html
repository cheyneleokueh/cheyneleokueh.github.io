Research研究

Prof. Fang-Chung Chen's organic electronics lab, NYCU Photonics, since my first semester. Each project is logged as it stands.大一上起於陳方中教授有機電子實驗室。照實記錄每個專題目前的狀態。

  1. Shockley–Queisser limit simulationShockley–Queisser 極限模擬與最佳化

    2025–26, Special Project I–II, presented at the lab meeting2025–26，光電專題研究（一）（二），於實驗室 meeting 報告

    A detailed-balance optimiser, applied to simulated underwater spectra to see how depth changes the best achievable efficiency.以細緻平衡極限建立最佳化工具，套用到水下模擬光譜，分析深度如何改變最佳轉換效率。

  2. A digital twin of a perovskite solar cell鈣鈦礦太陽能電池的數位分身

    2026, Special Project III, in progress2026，光電專題研究（三），進行中

    Drift-diffusion simulation in OghmaNano, then two ways back from a measured J–V curve to device parameters: optimisation fitting, and a conditional autoencoder trained on 5,000 simulated devices.以 OghmaNano 做 drift-diffusion 模擬，再用兩種方法從量測 J–V 曲線反推元件參數：最佳化擬合，以及用 5,000 顆模擬元件訓練的 conditional autoencoder。

    Public demo: the paper-trained model in the browser公開展示：在瀏覽器裡跑論文模型

  3. Carrier lifetime against shunt resistance載子壽命與並聯電阻的拉鋸

    2026, inconclusive2026，尚無定論

    On one measured curve, several parameter sets fit equally well, and the autoencoder did not beat brute-force search. Dark J–V and light-intensity measurements are next.只有一條量測曲線時，好幾組參數擬合得一樣好，autoencoder 也沒有贏過暴力搜尋。下一步是暗態 J–V 與不同光強的量測。

  4. NSTC undergraduate research grant, then a five-year master's國科會大專生研究計畫，接著五年碩

    2027, planned2027，規劃中

    A proposal to extend the digital twin into an efficiency optimiser, then NYCU's five-year master's track in the same lab.把數位分身延伸成效率最佳化工具的計畫，之後是同實驗室的五年碩學程。
