- Languages: home page and important pages carry an EN / 中文 toggle; course reviews are Chinese only (student view); the NZ page is English only.

## Product Principles

1. Research first: within one viewport a professor should know what he works on and where to read more.
2. Honest record: show wins, ongoing campaigns and setbacks plainly; nothing planned is shown as done.
3. Two homes, one voice: NZ and Taiwan/Japan influences meet in one coherent world, not a collage.
4. The world serves the reading: the playful map frames the page; facts stay fast to scan and readable.
