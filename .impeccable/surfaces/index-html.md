## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a sheet of Japanese manuscript paper (原稿用紙), and his record is marked the way a Japanese teacher marks returned work: 花丸 for a result, ○ done, △ partly, an open circle still being drawn, a dashed pencil circle for planned. It refuses the academic template (photo left, bio, interest bullets) and the game world it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Cream sheet (#f2efe8) with warm manuscript ruling, ink type, an indigo (#1e2b45) hanko stamp for the primary action, and a deep-gold pen for the marks; pencil grey for planned and secondary text. Dark theme is indigo night with a gold stamp. Shippori Mincho for display (a kanji subset for 郭紹安 and the status kanji), Source Sans 3 for text, Noto Sans TC for Chinese running text. Rows on the CV carry line numbers in a margin column. Palette and type come from the 本陣 commander study the user picked.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor reads who he is and what he researches, sees the red-pen marks, and can email him or open the CV. Below: three marked research projects and one planned, SPIE leadership, education and languages, teaching, contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The user's own photograph fills the hero (him on a balcony looking over fields and a town at golden hour), fading into the cream sheet on the left. Over the fade: 郭紹安 written vertically in three manuscript cells, Cheyne Kueh in Shippori Mincho at display size, one sentence on his research, then the indigo Email stamp, CV, GitHub and LinkedIn. On phones the photo sits on top and the name cells run horizontally above the English name.

FORM: Genkō yōshi manuscript sheet, position 7 of my ordered list, seed:4ad8f36773bbb1fe. Raises: numbered CV rows in the margin column (from the jackfield); 郭紹安 and Cheyne Kueh in equal balance (from city-pop); red reserved for live work and the primary action (from hand-processed film). Signature interaction: the red-pen marks draw themselves once when they scroll into view; drawn statically under reduced motion or without JS.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
