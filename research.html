Shockley–Queisser limit simulationShockley–Queisser 極限模擬與最佳化

2025–26, Special Project I–II2025–26，光電專題研究（一）（二）

Question問題
How does water depth change the best efficiency a solar cell can reach?水深如何改變太陽能電池可達到的最佳效率？
Method方法
A numerical optimiser built on the detailed-balance limit, run on simulated underwater spectra and checked against the lab's limit code.以細緻平衡極限建立數值最佳化工具，套用到水下模擬光譜，並與實驗室的極限程式對照。

A digital twin of a perovskite solar cell鈣鈦礦太陽能電池的數位分身

2026, Special Project III, in progress2026，光電專題研究（三），進行中

Question問題
Can one measured J–V curve reveal a cell's hidden parameters: carrier lifetime, mobility, ion density, series and shunt resistance?一條量測 J–V 曲線，能不能反推出元件的隱藏參數：載子壽命、遷移率、離子密度、串並聯電阻？
Method方法
A drift-diffusion twin in OghmaNano, then two ways back from the curve: direct optimisation fitting, and a conditional autoencoder trained on 5,000 simulated devices.以 OghmaNano 建立 drift-diffusion 數位分身，再用兩種方法從曲線反推：直接最佳化擬合，以及用 5,000 顆模擬元件訓練的 conditional autoencoder。
So far目前
The paper-trained model runs in the browser as a public demo.論文訓練的模型已做成可在瀏覽器執行的公開展示。 Open the repository打開程式庫

Carrier lifetime against shunt resistance載子壽命與並聯電阻的拉鋸

2026, inconclusive2026，尚無定論

Question問題
Can a single curve separate a short carrier lifetime from a leaky shunt?單一條曲線，分得出載子壽命短還是並聯電阻漏電嗎？
Finding發現
Not yet. Several parameter sets fit equally well, and the autoencoder's fit did not beat brute-force search.目前還不行。好幾組參數擬合得一樣好，autoencoder 的結果也沒有贏過暴力搜尋。
Next下一步
More evidence per device: dark J–V and light-intensity measurements.每顆元件需要更多證據：暗態 J–V 與不同光強的量測。
